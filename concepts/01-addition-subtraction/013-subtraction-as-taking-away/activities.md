# Physical Hands-On Activities: Subtraction as Taking Away

## Activity 1: The Hungry Monster 👾
* **Materials**: A cardboard box decorated as a "monster", 10 small plastic balls or counters.
* **Setup**: Place 7 counters in front of the monster.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Tell your child: *"The monster is hungry! Feed it 3 counters."*
  2. The child pushes 3 counters into the monster's mouth.
  3. Count how many counters remain outside the monster's mouth.
  4. Say the sentence together: *"7 take away 3 leaves 4!"*

---

## Activity 2: Bowling Pin Knockdown 🎳
* **Materials**: 6 plastic cups or toy bowling pins, a soft ball.
* **Setup**: Set up all 6 pins in a line.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Count all 6 standing pins together.
  2. Roll the ball to knock down some pins (e.g., 2 pins fall).
  3. Ask: *"How many pins are still standing?"* ($6 - 2 = 4$).
  4. Reset and play with different amounts up to 10.

---

## Activity 3: Penny Hide-and-Seek 🪙
* **Materials**: 5 coins or buttons, 1 opaque plastic cup.
* **Setup**: Place 5 coins on the table.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Have your child close their eyes while you cover 2 coins with the cup.
  2. The child opens their eyes, sees 3 coins remaining, and figures out how many coins were taken away ($5 - 3 = 2$).
