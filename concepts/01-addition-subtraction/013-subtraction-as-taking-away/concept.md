# Concept Blueprint: Subtraction as Taking Away

## 1. What the Child Should Understand
- **Separation (Taking Away)**: Removing a subset from a starting collection to find how many items remain. E.g., starting with 5 apples, eating 2 apples, leaving 3 apples.
- **The Minus Sign (-)**: Represents the operation of taking away or subtracting.
- **The Equals Sign (=)**: Indicates that the amount remaining after taking away is equal to the expression on the left ($5 - 2 = 3$).
- **Starting Set Importance**: Unlike addition, order matters in subtraction ($5 - 2 \neq 2 - 5$). You start with the total group and remove a part to find the other part.

---

## 2. What the Child Should Be Able to Do
- Start with a given collection, physically or visually remove a specified number of items, and count the remainder.
- Use a ten-frame workspace to clear slots corresponding to the subtracted amount.
- Translate a "taking away" narrative into a standard mathematical equation ($N - M = R$).

---

## 3. Success Criteria & Assessment Rubric
- **Level 1 (Beginning)**: Requires physical objects to remove one-by-one and recounts the remaining items from 1.
- **Level 2 (Developing)**: Can cross out or clear items visually on a ten-frame and state the remaining count without recounting from 1.
- **Level 3 (Mastered)**: Recognizes basic subtraction facts within 10 instantly using visual part-whole relationships.
