# Physical Hands-On Activities: Subtraction on Number Line

## Activity 1: Sidewalk Chalk Backward Hopscotch 🟩
* **Materials**: Sidewalk chalk, driveway or pavement space.
* **Setup**: Draw a long line numbered 0 through 10 with clear tick marks spaced 30cm apart.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Have your child stand on mark **7**.
  2. Call out: *"Subtract 3! Hop backward 3 times!"*
  3. The child hops backward: 6... 5... 4!
  4. Have them write the equation on the chalk board: $7 - 3 = 4$.

---

## Activity 2: Tape Measure Toy Car Race 🏎️
* **Materials**: A measuring tape or printed paper number line (0–10), 1 toy car.
* **Setup**: Lay out the tape measure on the floor or table.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Place the toy car on mark **9**.
  2. Draw a card showing a subtraction rule (e.g., "- 4").
  3. Roll the car backward 4 notches along the tape measure.
  4. Ask: *"What mark did the car park on?"* ($9 - 4 = 5$).

---

## Activity 3: Number Line Bead Slide 📿
* **Materials**: A shoelace or string with 10 wooden beads, numbered 0 to 10.
* **Setup**: Thread 10 beads onto the lace.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Push all 10 beads to the right side.
  2. Have your child slide 4 beads backward to the left.
  3. Count how many beads remain on the right side.
