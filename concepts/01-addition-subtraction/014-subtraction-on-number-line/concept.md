# Concept Blueprint: Subtraction on Number Line

## 1. What the Child Should Understand
- **Backward Hops**: Subtraction means moving to the left (backward) on a number line toward 0. E.g., starting at 7 and hopping back 3 steps lands on 4 ($7 - 3 = 4$).
- **Starting Position**: The first number (minuend) marks the starting position on the scale.
- **Hop Count vs Notch Count**: Count the number of spaces/hops jumped, not the starting mark line.

---

## 2. What the Child Should Be Able to Do
- Locate the starting number on a 0–10 number line.
- Execute backward hops corresponding to the number being subtracted.
- Identify the landing position as the final answer/difference.

---

## 3. Success Criteria & Assessment Rubric
- **Level 1 (Beginning)**: Needs physical finger tracing along the scale line and recounts every notch from 0.
- **Level 2 (Developing)**: Can place finger at the starting position and jump backward step-by-step to find the answer.
- **Level 3 (Mastered)**: Instantly visualizes the backward leap on the scale line to solve equations within 10.
