# Common Misconceptions: Subtraction on Number Line

### 1. Counting the Starting Notch (The Off-By-One Trap)
* **The Trap**: When subtracting $6 - 2$, the child points to 6 and says *"1"*, then moves to 5 and says *"2"*, landing on 5.
* **Why it happens**: The child counts the starting line instead of the jump/distance moved.
* **Parent Verbal Prompt**: *"Start at 6! Don't count 1 until your foot actually takes a hop backward to 5."*

---

### 2. Hopping Forward (Direction Confusion)
* **The Trap**: When asked to solve $7 - 3$, the child hops to the right and lands on 10.
* **Why it happens**: Habit from addition (counting on) or confusing left/right directions on the scale line.
* **Parent Verbal Prompt**: *"Subtraction makes numbers smaller! Walk backward toward 0."*

---

### 3. Landing Off-Notch
* **The Trap**: Stopping in the space between tick marks rather than squarely on the target numeral.
* **Why it happens**: Imprecise finger tracing or dragging.
* **Parent Verbal Prompt**: *"Land right on top of the number marker!"*
