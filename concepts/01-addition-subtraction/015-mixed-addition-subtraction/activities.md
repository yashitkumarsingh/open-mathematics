# Physical Hands-On Activities: Mixed Addition & Subtraction

## Activity 1: The Domino Fact Family House 🏠
* **Materials**: Standard double-six dominoes, paper, pencil.
* **Setup**: Draw a simple house shape with three rooms.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Pick a domino (e.g., 4 dots on one side, 2 dots on the other = 6 dots total).
  2. Write the 3 numbers in the house roof: **6, 4, 2**.
  3. Have your child write the 4 equations of the family:
     - $4 + 2 = 6$
     - $2 + 4 = 6$
     - $6 - 2 = 4$
     - $6 - 4 = 2$

---

## Activity 2: Flashcard Operator Toss 🎲
* **Materials**: Soft sponge or die marked with $+$ on 3 sides and $-$ on 3 sides, number cards 1–10.
* **Setup**: Place two number cards on the table (e.g. 8 and 3).
* **How to Play**:
  1. Roll the operator die.
  2. If $+$ comes up, add them ($8 + 3 = 11$ or $5 + 3 = 8$).
  3. If $-$ comes up, subtract the smaller from the larger ($8 - 3 = 5$).
  4. Perform the action using counters on a ten-frame.

---

## Activity 3: Mystery Box Reversal 🎁
* **Materials**: 1 shoebox, 10 plastic counters or coins.
* **Setup**: Hide some counters inside the shoebox.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Show 4 counters outside the box and say: *"Together with what's inside, we have 9 counters total!"*
  2. Have your child calculate: $9 - 4 = 5$ to guess how many are inside the box.
  3. Open the box to verify!
