# Concept Blueprint: Mixed Addition & Subtraction

## 1. What the Child Should Understand
- **Fact Families (Inverse Relationship)**: Addition and subtraction are inverse operations. E.g., if $4 + 3 = 7$, then $7 - 3 = 4$ and $7 - 4 = 3$.
- **Symbol Discrimination**: Distinguishing between the plus sign ($+$) which means combining/increasing and the minus sign ($-$) which means taking away/decreasing.
- **Part-Whole Equivalence**: A whole number (7) can be split into two parts (4 and 3) or built by combining those same two parts.

---

## 2. What the Child Should Be Able to Do
- Look at an operation symbol ($+$ or $-$) and apply the correct visual manipulation (adding or removing counters).
- Identify missing numbers in simple fact family triangles.
- Solve mixed practice sets containing both addition and subtraction within 10.

---

## 3. Success Criteria & Assessment Rubric
- **Level 1 (Beginning)**: Requires visual prompts to distinguish $+$ from $-$ and recounts sets from 1 every time.
- **Level 2 (Developing)**: Can correctly select addition vs subtraction actions, but takes extra time to switch mental modes between problems.
- **Level 3 (Mastered)**: Fluently transitions between addition and subtraction problems within 10, utilizing fact family knowledge.
