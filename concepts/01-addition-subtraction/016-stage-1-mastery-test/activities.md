# Physical Hands-On Activities: Stage 1 Mixed Mastery Test

## Activity 1: Stage 1 Board Game Relay 🎲
* **Materials**: Paper board game track with 10 squares, 1 pawn, 1 die, 5 problem cards.
* **Setup**: Place pawn at square 0.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Draw a card (e.g. $4 + 3$, $8 - 2$, $5 + 4$, $9 - 5$, $6 + 2$).
  2. Have your child solve the problem.
  3. If correct, roll the die and move forward along the track!
  4. Reach square 10 to win the Stage 1 Champion Trophy!

## Activity 2: Flashcard Speed Match (Calm Mode) 🃏
* **Materials**: 10 index cards with addition and subtraction equations.
* **Setup**: Place 2 answer bowls marked **"Less than 5"** and **"5 or More"**.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Have your child pick a card (e.g. $7 - 3 = 4$).
  2. Solve the equation and place the card into the correct answer bowl.
  3. Sort all 10 cards to complete the game.
