# Concept Blueprint: Stage 1 Mixed Mastery Test

## 1. What the Child Should Understand
- **Stage 1 Synthesis**: Addition and subtraction within 10 are foundational operations that connect physical set manipulations, scale movements, and fact family logic.
- **Operational Versatility**: Fluid transition between combining items ($+$), hopping forward, taking items away ($-$), hopping backward on a number line, and missing-part fact families.

---

## 2. What the Child Should Be Able to Do
- Independently solve a randomized 5-question test set covering all Stage 1 problem formats.
- Self-correct errors using visual hints without fear of punishment or red failure banners.
- Earn Stage 1 Mastery status upon achieving a score of 4 or 5 correct answers.

---

## 3. Success Criteria & Assessment Rubric
- **Level 1 (Beginning)**: Requires step-by-step assistance and physical objects to solve mixed addition/subtraction problems.
- **Level 2 (Developing)**: Solves 3 out of 5 items independently, occasionally needing reminders when switching between $+$ and $-$.
- **Level 3 (Mastered)**: Scores 4+ out of 5 independently, demonstrating Stage 1 Addition & Subtraction fluency.
