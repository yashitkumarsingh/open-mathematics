# Common Misconceptions: Stage 1 Mixed Mastery Test

### 1. Misreading the Operational Sign Under Test Conditions
* **The Trap**: The child adds when seeing a minus sign or vice versa due to distraction.
* **Why it happens**: Habitual addition responses when reviewing multiple questions in sequence.
* **Parent Verbal Prompt**: *"Pause before answering! Point to the sign with your finger. Is it a Plus (+) or a Minus (-)?"*

---

### 2. Off-By-One Errors on Scale Hops
* **The Trap**: Counting the starting line as step 1 when hopping backward or forward on a number line.
* **Why it happens**: Forgetting that a hop represents the distance moved, not the starting mark.
* **Parent Verbal Prompt**: *"Count the hops! Jump first, then count '1'."*

---

### 3. Test Anxiety & Hesitation
* **The Trap**: The child feels nervous seeing the word "Test".
* **Why it happens**: Fear of getting an answer wrong.
* **Parent Verbal Prompt**: *"This is just a fun game to show what you've learned! There are no timers."*
