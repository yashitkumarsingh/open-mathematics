# Parent Guide: Stage 1 Mixed Mastery Test

## 2-Minute Overview
Congratulations on reaching the **Stage 1 Mastery Test**! This milestone checks your child's overall understanding of addition and subtraction within 10 before progressing to Stage 2 (Place Value & Two-Digit Numbers). It weaves together combining items, counting on, taking away, number line hops, and fact families.

---

## 5-Minute Everyday Activity: Parent Review Challenge 🏆
1. Prepare a quick 5-question game using household objects:
   - *Item 1 (Combining)*: Merge 3 red spoons and 4 blue spoons ($3 + 4 = 7$).
   - *Item 2 (Counting On)*: Start at 5 and count on 2 steps ($5 + 2 = 7$).
   - *Item 3 (Taking Away)*: Start with 6 biscuits, eat 2 ($6 - 2 = 4$).
   - *Item 4 (Number Line Hop)*: Start at 8 on a ruler, hop back 3 ($8 - 3 = 5$).
   - *Item 5 (Fact Family)*: Ask *"If 4 + 2 = 6, what is 6 - 2?"* ($4$).
2. Praise your child's effort regardless of mistakes!

---

## 10-Minute Extension Challenge: Stage 1 Celebration 🥳
- Once your child passes the test on screen, award them a physical high-five or sticker!
- Ask them to explain their favorite way to solve math problems (e.g. using ten-frames or hopping on number lines).

---

## Parent Observation Guide
- **What to look for**: Is your child confident switching between $+$ and $-$ signs?
- **Encouragement Tip**: If your child makes a mistake, encourage them to use the built-in hint button or try again calmly.

---

## What NOT to Do
- 🚫 **Do not make it stressful**: This is a friendly checkpoint, not a high-stakes exam.
- 🚫 **Do not introduce time limits**: Allow as much time as your child needs to think.
