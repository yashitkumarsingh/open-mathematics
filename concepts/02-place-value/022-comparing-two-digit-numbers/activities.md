# Hands-on Activities: Comparing Numbers

Use physical games to build numerical comparative intuition.

---

## Game 1: Block Wars (Rods and Units Battle)
* **Goal**: Understand comparative values by direct physical matching.
* **Items needed**: Straws (units) and rubber-banded bundles of 10 (tens), or LEGO blocks (ten-rods and single studs).
* **How to play**:
  1. Split a deck of number cards (e.g. 10 to 30) between two players.
  2. Each player flips a card (e.g. Player A flips **21**, Player B flips **18**).
  3. Both players build their number using their rods and units.
  4. Compare the two builds side-by-side:
     - Player A sees they have 2 Ten rods.
     - Player B sees they have 1 Ten rod and some units.
     - The player with the larger representation wins both cards.
  5. The player with the most cards at the end wins!

## Game 2: The Crocodile Trap
* **Goal**: Memorise the correct usage of inequality symbols.
* **Items needed**: A large sheet of paper, green cardstock or plastic spoons to act as a crocodile mouth (V shape), and small treats (raisins, crackers, or coins).
* **How to play**:
  1. Place 14 raisins on the left side of the paper and 20 raisins on the right side.
  2. Have the child position the green alligator mouth V between the two groups.
  3. Make sure the mouth is open wide toward the 20 raisins.
  4. Write the corresponding mathematical sentence below: $14 < 20$.
