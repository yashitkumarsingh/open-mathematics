# Concept Blueprint: Comparing Two-Digit Numbers

## 1. What the Child Should Understand
- **Tens Priority**: A larger number of Tens rods always beats any number of Ones units (e.g. 2 tens and 1 one is larger than 1 ten and 9 ones, because 20 is more than 10).
- **Symbol Meaning**: The open side of the comparison mouth points to the larger value ($24 > 19$).
- **Tie-Breaker Column**: If the tens are equal, look at the ones column to decide.

---

## 2. What the Child Should Be Able to Do
- Compare side-by-side blocks and identify which represents the greater or lesser value.
- Fill in the comparison symbols ($<$, $>$, $=$) between two numbers.

---

## 3. Success Criteria & Assessment Rubric
- **Level 1 (Beginning)**: Compares by counting all ones from 1 (e.g., counting 23 blocks and then 21 blocks to see which is more).
- **Level 2 (Developing)**: Confuses values due to "ones bias" (e.g. choosing 19 over 21 because 9 is larger than 1).
- **Level 3 (Mastered)**: Mentally compares tens first, resolves equal tens by comparing ones, and matches with correct inequality symbols.
