# Hands-on Activities: Ten More, Ten Less

Use these tactile games to reinforce place value mental addition/subtraction.

---

## Game 1: The Magic Ten-Ruler Jump
* **Goal**: Visualize adding or subtracting 10 on a scale.
* **Items needed**: A long measuring tape (or paper number line up to 50), and a toy figure.
* **How to play**:
  1. Place the figure on **13**.
  2. Say: *"Find 10 more than 13! Take a magic jump!"*
  3. Instead of moving step-by-step, have the child make one giant leap forward to land on **23**.
  4. Point out the digit pattern: *"Look! 13 has a 3. 23 has a 3. The 3 stayed the same, but the tens digit went up by 1!"*
  5. Try jumps backward for 10 less: *"Stand on 35. Take a magic jump backward! Where do you land? (25)."*

---

## Game 2: Flash and Add
* **Goal**: Build rapid recognition of +10 and -10 patterns.
* **Items needed**: Two-digit number cards (10 to 30), and two special operational cards: **"+10"** and **"-10"**.
* **How to play**:
  1. Draw a number card (e.g. **28**).
  2. Flip an operational card (e.g. **"-10"**).
  3. The child must say the answer as fast as they can: *"18!"*
  4. Build it with rods to verify if they get stuck or make a mistake.
