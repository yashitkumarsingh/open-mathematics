# Concept Blueprint: Ten More, Ten Less

## 1. What the Child Should Understand
- **Tens Change, Ones Conserve**: Adding 10 is equivalent to adding 1 Ten rod, which increases the tens digit by 1. The ones digit does not change.
- **Mental Jump**: The ability to add or subtract 10 mentally without counting individual ones blocks from the start.
- **Reverse Action**: Subtracting 10 removes 1 Ten rod, decreasing the tens digit by 1.

---

## 2. What the Child Should Be Able to Do
- Given a base-ten representation, mentally state 10 more or 10 less.
- Interact with base-ten controls to add or remove exactly 1 Ten rod to solve "10 more" or "10 less" challenges.

---

## 3. Success Criteria & Assessment Rubric
- **Level 1 (Beginning)**: Counts by ones to find 10 more (e.g. starts at 15 and counts *"16, 17... 25"*).
- **Level 2 (Developing)**: Knows that the number changes, but sometimes modifies the ones place by mistake (e.g. adding 10 to 15 and stating 20 or 26).
- **Level 3 (Mastered)**: Instantly adds or subtracts from the tens place mentally (e.g., $15 \rightarrow 25$ or $27 \rightarrow 17$) keeping the ones place identical, and manipulates block rods correctly.
