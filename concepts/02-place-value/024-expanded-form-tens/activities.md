# Physical Hands-On Activities: Expanded Form & Tens Decompositions

## Activity 1: Straw Bundle Expansion 🌾
* **Materials**: 10 bundles of 10 drinking straws (tied with rubber bands), 10 single straws.
* **Setup**: Place bundles on the left and loose straws on the right.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Call out a number (e.g. **36**).
  2. Have your child lay out 3 bundles ($30$) and 6 single straws ($6$).
  3. Have them say: *"30 plus 6 equals 36!"*

---

## Activity 2: Expanded Form Card Puzzle 🧩
* **Materials**: Index cards with numbers (e.g. 50, 7, 57; 80, 2, 82).
* **Setup**: Mix up the tens cards, ones cards, and standard number cards on the table.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Match the tens card ($50$) and ones card ($7$) to the standard card ($57$).
  2. Create a 3-card equation chain: **50 + 7 = 57**.

---

## Activity 3: The Missing Value Detective 🕵️
* **Materials**: Paper and pencil.
* **Setup**: Write incomplete equations (e.g. $45 = \text{\_\_} + 5$ or $63 = 60 + \text{\_\_}$).
* **How to Play**:
  1. Your child plays the "Math Detective" and fills in the missing expanded value.
  2. Reward each solved mystery with a detective badge or star!
