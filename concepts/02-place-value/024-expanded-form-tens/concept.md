# Concept Blueprint: Expanded Form & Tens Decompositions

## 1. What the Child Should Understand
- **Value vs Digit**: The digit `3` in `34` represents a value of **30** (3 tens), while the digit `4` represents **4** (4 ones).
- **Expanded Addition**: Every 2-digit number can be written as a sum of tens and ones ($34 = 30 + 4$).
- **Decomposition**: Breaking a whole quantity into tens value and ones value helps mental addition and subtraction.

---

## 2. What the Child Should Be Able to Do
- Write any 2-digit number up to 99 in expanded form ($58 = 50 + 8$).
- Recombine an expanded form expression ($70 + 6$) back into standard form ($76$).
- Match `<base-ten-blocks>` visualizations to expanded equations.

---

## 3. Success Criteria & Assessment Rubric
- **Level 1 (Beginning)**: Can count individual blocks, but writes $34$ as $3 + 4$ instead of $30 + 4$.
- **Level 2 (Developing)**: Can write expanded form when looking at base-ten blocks, but hesitates when converting raw digits.
- **Level 3 (Mastered)**: Fluently expands and recombines two-digit numbers up to 99 in standard and missing-part formats.
