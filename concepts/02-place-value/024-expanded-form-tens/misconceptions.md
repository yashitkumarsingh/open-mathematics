# Common Misconceptions: Expanded Form & Tens Decompositions

### 1. Writing Digit Values Instead of Place Values ($34 = 3 + 4$)
* **The Trap**: Writing $34 = 3 + 4$ instead of $34 = 30 + 4$.
* **Why it happens**: The child treats digits literally without assigning their place value multiplier.
* **Parent Verbal Prompt**: *"That 3 is sitting in the Tens chair! 3 tens is equal to 30."*

---

### 2. Swapping Tens and Ones ($42 = 20 + 4$)
* **The Trap**: Reversing the order of expanded terms.
* **Why it happens**: Confusion between which digit represents tens vs ones.
* **Parent Verbal Prompt**: *"Look at the first number—how many bundles of 10 do we have?"*

---

### 3. Recombination Confusion ($60 + 8 = 608$)
* **The Trap**: Writing $60 + 8 = 608$ by appending digits side-by-side.
* **Why it happens**: Seeing the zero in 60 and placing 8 right after it instead of covering the zero.
* **Parent Verbal Prompt**: *"The 8 ones slide right over the 0! 6 tens and 8 ones makes 68."*
