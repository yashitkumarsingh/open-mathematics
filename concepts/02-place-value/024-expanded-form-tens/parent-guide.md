# Parent Guide: Expanded Form & Tens Decompositions

## 2-Minute Overview
Expanded form helps children see what numbers are *made of*. When your child sees the number **47**, expanded form stretches it out to show **40 + 7**. Understanding that the "4" actually stands for **40** is the single most important secret to mastering multi-digit math!

---

## 5-Minute Everyday Activity: Stretch the Number Card 💳
1. Take two sticky notes or index cards.
2. Write **40** on the left card and **7** on the right card.
3. Slide the **7** card over the zero of **40** to make **47**!
4. Ask your child to pull the cards apart ("stretch it out!") and say: *"47 is 40 plus 7!"*
5. Practice with other two-digit numbers (e.g. 25, 63, 81).

---

## 10-Minute Extension Challenge: Price Tag Expansion 🏷️
- Look at item prices around the house (e.g. a book costing $24 or a toy costing $58).
- Ask your child to act as the store manager and state the price in expanded form: *"That book is 20 dollars plus 4 dollars!"*

---

## Parent Observation Guide
- **What to look for**: Does your child write $34 = 3 + 4$? Remind them that the first digit represents bundles of 10, so 3 tens equals **30**, not 3!

---

## What NOT to Do
- 🚫 **Do not skip the zero**: Emphasize that the tens place value ends in a zero (e.g. 5 tens = 50).
- 🚫 **Do not rush to three-digit numbers**: Ensure complete comfort with two-digit expansion up to 99 first.
