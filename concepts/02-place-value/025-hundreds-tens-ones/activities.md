# Physical Hands-On Activities: Hundreds, Tens, and Ones

## Activity 1: The 3-Column Place Value Mat 📄
* **Materials**: Large sheet of paper divided into 3 columns (**Hundreds | Tens | Ones**), paper squares for flats/rods/units.
* **Setup**: Place the mat on the table.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Call out a 3-digit number (e.g. **342**).
  2. Have your child place 3 flats in Hundreds, 4 rods in Tens, and 2 units in Ones.
  3. Write the number underneath: **342**.

---

## Activity 2: Roll-a-Hundred Game 🎲
* **Materials**: 3 dice (different colors: Red = Hundreds, Blue = Tens, Green = Ones).
* **Setup**: Roll all 3 dice simultaneously.
* **How to Play**:
  1. If Red=4, Blue=2, Green=5 $\rightarrow$ Number is **425**.
  2. Have your child state the expanded form: *"400 + 20 + 5 = 425!"*
  3. Highest number after 5 rolls wins!

---

## Activity 3: Zero Placeholder Mystery 🕵️
* **Materials**: Number cards with zeroes (e.g. 104, 308, 502).
* **Setup**: Show the number 308.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Ask: *"How many Hundreds? 3! How many Tens? 0! How many Ones? 8!"*
  2. Build it with blocks to show why the zero is essential to keep 3 in the Hundreds seat.
