# Concept Blueprint: Hundreds, Tens, and Ones

## 1. What the Child Should Understand
- **The Hundreds Place**: 10 rods of Ten combine to form 1 flat of **Hundred** ($10 \times 10 = 100$).
- **3-Digit Place Value**: In a 3-digit number like `243`:
  - `2` represents **2 Hundreds** ($200$)
  - `4` represents **4 Tens** ($40$)
  - `3` represents **3 Ones** ($3$)
- **Expanded 3-Digit Form**: $243 = 200 + 40 + 3$.

---

## 2. What the Child Should Be Able to Do
- Identify the value of any digit in a 3-digit number up to 999.
- Represent 3-digit numbers using base-ten blocks (flats, rods, units).
- Expand and recombine 3-digit expressions ($300 + 50 + 4 = 354$).

---

## 3. Success Criteria & Assessment Rubric
- **Level 1 (Beginning)**: Can identify 2-digit numbers, but gets confused by the Hundreds place position.
- **Level 2 (Developing)**: Can count Hundreds flats, but occasionally swaps Tens and Ones values.
- **Level 3 (Mastered)**: Fluently reads, builds, and expands 3-digit numbers up to 999.
