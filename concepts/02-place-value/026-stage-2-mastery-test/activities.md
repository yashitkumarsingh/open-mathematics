# Physical Hands-On Activities: Stage 2 Mixed Mastery Test

## Activity 1: Stage 2 Place Value Race 🏁
* **Materials**: 2 sets of number cards (0-9), place value mat (Hundreds, Tens, Ones).
* **Setup**: Divide cards into two piles.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Draw 3 cards to form a 3-digit number (e.g. 4, 7, 2 $\rightarrow$ 472).
  2. Have your child state:
     - Expanded form ($400 + 70 + 2$)
     - 10 more ($482$)
     - 10 less ($462$)
  3. Score 1 point for each correct answer!

## Activity 2: Alligator Symbol Showdown 🐊
* **Materials**: Cardboard alligator mouth ($>$ and $<$) and $=$ sign.
* **Setup**: Place two number cards side by side (e.g. **68** and **86**).
* **How to Play**:
  1. Have your child point the alligator mouth to eat the larger number!
  2. Explain why: *"8 tens is larger than 6 tens!"*
