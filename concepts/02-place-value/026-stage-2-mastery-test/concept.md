# Concept Blueprint: Stage 2 Mixed Mastery Test

## 1. What the Child Should Understand
- **Stage 2 Synthesis**: Numbers up to 999 are built from Hundreds, Tens, and Ones ($243 = 200 + 40 + 3$).
- **Place Value Properties**: Comparing multi-digit numbers starts from the highest place value seat (Hundreds $\rightarrow$ Tens $\rightarrow$ Ones).
- **Mental Ten Jump**: Adding or subtracting 10 changes only the Tens place digit ($47 + 10 = 57$).

---

## 2. What the Child Should Be Able to Do
- Independently solve a 5-question test set covering all Stage 2 problem formats.
- Convert between base-ten block visual representations, expanded equations, and standard numerals.
- Earn Stage 2 Mastery status upon achieving a score of 4 or 5 correct answers.

---

## 3. Success Criteria & Assessment Rubric
- **Level 1 (Beginning)**: Requires block counters and step-by-step parent support to compare or expand 3-digit numbers.
- **Level 2 (Developing)**: Solves 3 out of 5 items independently, occasionally missing zero placeholders.
- **Level 3 (Mastered)**: Scores 4+ out of 5 independently, demonstrating Stage 2 Place Value fluency.
