# Common Misconceptions: Stage 2 Mixed Mastery Test

### 1. Right-to-Left Comparison Error
* **The Trap**: Comparing 34 and 43 by looking at the rightmost digit first ($4 > 3$).
* **Why it happens**: Habitual reading order confusion when focusing on ones before tens.
* **Parent Verbal Prompt**: *"Always check the biggest seat first! Compare the tens: 4 tens is bigger than 3 tens."*

---

### 2. Zero Placeholder Confusion ($305 = 35$)
* **The Trap**: Dropping zero when expanding or building 305.
* **Why it happens**: Forgetting that 0 holds the tens column open.
* **Parent Verbal Prompt**: *"0 is the place keeper! It holds the tens chair so 3 can sit in the hundreds chair."*

---

### 3. Adding 10 to Both Tens and Ones ($34 + 10 = 44$)
* **The Trap**: Accidentally adding 1 to the ones place ($34 + 10 = 45$).
* **Why it happens**: Counting on by 1 instead of jumping by 10.
* **Parent Verbal Prompt**: *"Jump by a full bundle of 10! Only the tens digit changes."*
