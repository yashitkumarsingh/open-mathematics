# Parent Guide: Stage 2 Mixed Mastery Test

## 2-Minute Overview
Congratulations on reaching the **Stage 2 Mastery Test**! This milestone reviews your child's complete understanding of place value, 2-digit and 3-digit numbers, expanded form ($30 + 4 = 34$), ten-more/ten-less jumps, and comparison symbols ($>$, $<$, $=$). Passing this test confirms readiness for Stage 3 (Multiplication & Division).

---

## 5-Minute Everyday Activity: Stage 2 Checkup Game 🏆
1. Prepare 5 quick verbal or paper questions:
   - *Q1 (Tens & Ones)*: How many tens and ones in 47? (4 tens, 7 ones)
   - *Q2 (Comparing)*: Which is larger: 52 or 25? (52)
   - *Q3 (Ten Jump)*: What is 10 more than 63? (73)
   - *Q4 (Expanded Form)*: What is 80 + 6? (86)
   - *Q5 (3-Digit Place Value)*: What does the 3 mean in 354? (300)
2. Praise your child's progress!

---

## 10-Minute Extension Challenge: Stage 2 Master Badge 🎖️
- Once your child passes the test on screen, award them a physical "Place Value Master" badge or star!
- Ask them to explain how they compared two 3-digit numbers.

---

## Parent Observation Guide
- **What to look for**: Is your child comparing the leftmost digit first when looking at $>$, $<$, $=$?
- **Encouragement Tip**: Remind them there are no countdown clocks or time limits.

---

## What NOT to Do
- 🚫 **Do not make it stressful**: Keep the tone encouraging and playful.
- 🚫 **Do not punish wrong answers**: Use hints to guide thinking.
