# Hands-on Physical Activities: Skip Counting (2, 5, 10)

## Activity 1: Sidewalk Frog Jumps
* **Target Concept**: Visualizing equal jumps of 2, 5, or 10 on a number scale.
* **Materials Needed**: Outdoor chalk.
* **Setup**: Draw a chalk line numbered 0 to 20 on the sidewalk with tick marks.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Have the child stand on **0**.
  2. Call out a jump rule: *"Frog hops of 2!"*
  3. The child hops from 0 to 2, shouting *"2!"*, then to 4 shouting *"4!"*, and so on up to 20.
  4. Repeat with giant kangaroo hops of **5** ($0 \rightarrow 5 \rightarrow 10 \rightarrow 15 \rightarrow 20$).

---

## Activity 2: High-Five Counting
* **Target Concept**: Skip counting by 5s using hand fingers.
* **Materials Needed**: Paper outlines of traced hands (cut out) or family members' hands.
* **Setup**: Lay paper hands in a line on the floor.
* **How to Play**:
  1. High-five each paper hand while counting out loud: *"5! 10! 15! 20! 25!"*
  2. Turn hands upside down to hide the fingers and practice recalling the numbers from memory.

---

## Activity 3: Ten-Bundle Train
* **Target Concept**: Skip counting by 10s with physical bundles.
* **Materials Needed**: 50 popsicle sticks or straws, rubber bands.
* **Setup**: Make 5 bundles of 10 sticks each.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Place bundles down one by one onto a table.
  2. Each time a bundle lands, count up by 10s: *"10... 20... 30... 40... 50!"*
  3. Remove 2 bundles and ask how many remain by counting back by 10s.
