# Concept Blueprint: Skip Counting (2, 5, 10)

## 1. What the Child Should Understand
- **Equal Steps**: Skip counting means adding the same number repeatedly ($2+2+2...$, $5+5+5...$, $10+10+10...$).
- **Visual Rhythm & Patterns**: Counting by 2s lands on even numbers; counting by 5s creates a pattern ending in 5 and 0; counting by 10s increases the tens place while keeping ones at 0.
- **Foundation for Multiplication**: Skip counting $3$ jumps of $5$ lands on $15$, which directly models $3 \times 5 = 15$.

---

## 2. What the Child Should Be Able to Do
- Complete missing numbers in a skip counting sequence (e.g. $2, 4, \dots, 8, 10$).
- Jump along a number line in increments of 2, 5, or 10 to reach target goals.
- Identify whether a given number sequence is counting by 2s, 5s, or 10s.

---

## 3. Success Criteria & Assessment Rubric
- **Level 1 (Beginning)**: Counts by 1s between steps (e.g. *"2... 3, 4! 5, 6!"*).
- **Level 2 (Developing)**: Remembers patterns for 10s and 5s, but stumbles on higher jumps for 2s (e.g. past 12).
- **Level 3 (Mastered)**: Instantly skip counts by 2s, 5s, and 10s with fluency, predicting the next number in sequences without intermediate counting.
