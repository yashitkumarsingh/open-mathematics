# Hands-on Physical Activities: Equal Groups

## Activity 1: Muffin Tin Groups
* **Target Concept**: Building equal groups in physical containers.
* **Materials Needed**: A 6-cup muffin tin, 24 marbles or dried beans.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Give the child 4 cups of the muffin tin.
  2. Challenge them to put **3 beans** in each cup.
  3. Ask: *"How many cups?"* (4) *"How many in each?"* (3) *"What is 4 groups of 3?"* ($3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12$).
  4. Change the challenge to 2 cups with 6 beans each and compare totals!

---

## Activity 2: Egg Carton Array Bundling
* **Target Concept**: Connecting groups to total counts.
* **Materials Needed**: Cut-up egg cartons (sections of 2, 3, or 4 cups), small toys.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Lay out three 2-cup egg carton strips.
  2. Place 1 toy in each cup.
  3. Count by 2s: *"2, 4, 6 items total in 3 groups of 2!"*

---

## Activity 3: The Equal Share Party
* **Target Concept**: Ensuring groups are strictly equal before multiplying.
* **Materials Needed**: 3 stuffed animals, 15 play-money coins or pretend cookies.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Deal out cookies to the 3 toys so each gets 5 cookies.
  2. Have your child verify that the share is fair (equal groups).
  3. Calculate the total cookies using repeated addition: $5 + 5 + 5 = 15$.
