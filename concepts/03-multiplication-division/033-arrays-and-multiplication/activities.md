# Hands-on Physical Activities: Arrays & Multiplication

## Activity 1: Graph Paper Array City
* **Target Concept**: Building visual grid arrays and writing multiplication math sentences.
* **Materials Needed**: Grid paper, colored markers.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Have your child draw rectangular "skyscrapers" on graph paper by outlining grid squares.
  2. For a building that is 4 squares wide and 5 squares tall, label it: $4 \times 5 = 20 \text{ windows}$.
  3. Color the buildings to create a mathematical city skyline!

---

## Activity 2: Cheez-It Array Feast
* **Target Concept**: Constructing arrays physically with square snacks.
* **Materials Needed**: Square crackers (Cheez-Its, square pretzels, or cereal squares).
* **How to Play**:
  1. Give your child 12 square crackers.
  2. Ask them to build a rectangle with 3 rows of 4 crackers.
  3. Count total crackers: $3 \text{ rows} \times 4 \text{ columns} = 12$.
  4. Eat one row ($1 \times 4 = 4$) and calculate the remaining array!

---

## Activity 3: Spin-an-Array Game
* **Target Concept**: Fast visual creation of arrays.
* **Materials Needed**: Two dice (or spinner), small counters or LEGO bricks.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Roll two dice (e.g., a 3 and a 4).
  2. The first die tells how many rows to make (3).
  3. The second die tells how many columns to make (4).
  4. Build the array with LEGO bricks and shout out the total!
