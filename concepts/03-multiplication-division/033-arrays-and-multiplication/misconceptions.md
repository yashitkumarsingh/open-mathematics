# Common Misconceptions: Arrays & Multiplication

## 1. Row / Column Vocabulary Confusion
* **The Thinking Trap**: Swapping rows (horizontal) and columns (vertical).
* **Why it Happens**: Rows and columns are abstract spatial terms often introduced around the same time.
* **Parent Verbal Correction Prompt**:
  > *"Think of a movie theater row — you sit side-by-side from left to right! Think of tall stone columns on a building — they stand straight up and down!"*

---

## 2. Corner Item Double-Counting
* **The Thinking Trap**: Counting the top-left item twice — once for the top row and once for the first column — resulting in an extra count.
* **Why it Happens**: Misunderstanding that a single item belongs to both a row and a column simultaneously.
* **Parent Verbal Correction Prompt**:
  > *"That star belongs to Row 1 AND Column 1 at the exact same time! You only count it once when totaling the array."*

---

## 3. Disorganized Grid Alignment
* **The Thinking Trap**: Placing items out of alignment when building arrays, breaking the visual row/column structure.
* **Why it Happens**: Fine-motor spacing difficulties when placing physical counters without grid lines.
* **Parent Verbal Correction Prompt**:
  > *"Let's line up our blocks like marching soldiers! Every block must line up directly under the one above it."*
