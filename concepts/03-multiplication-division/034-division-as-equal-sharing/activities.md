# Hands-on Physical Activities: Division as Equal Sharing

## Activity 1: The Cookie Sharing Party
* **Target Concept**: Equal distribution dealt one by one.
* **Materials Needed**: 12 paper circles (pretend cookies), 3 stuffed animals.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Set out 3 stuffed animals.
  2. Hand your child 12 paper cookies.
  3. Tell them to deal out the cookies one by one so every animal gets a fair share.
  4. Count each animal's pile: *"Each animal gets 4 cookies! $12 \div 3 = 4$."*

---

## Activity 2: Playing Card Deal
* **Target Concept**: Building dealing fluency and group verification.
* **Materials Needed**: A deck of playing cards (use 20 cards).
* **How to Play**:
  1. Set up 4 players (family members or toy figures).
  2. Give your child a stack of 20 cards.
  3. Have them deal out the full deck evenly.
  4. Each player counts their hand: *"Everyone has 5 cards! $20 \div 4 = 5$."*

---

## Activity 3: Coin Bank Sorting
* **Target Concept**: Connecting division to inverse multiplication.
* **Materials Needed**: 15 coins or buttons, 5 small cups.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Ask your child to share 15 coins into 5 cups.
  2. After dealing 3 coins to each cup, ask: *"5 cups with 3 coins equals how many?"* ($5 \times 3 = 15$).
  3. Reverse it: *"So 15 coins shared into 5 cups equals...?"* ($15 \div 5 = 3$).
