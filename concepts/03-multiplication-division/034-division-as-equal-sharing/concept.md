# Concept Blueprint: Division as Equal Sharing

## 1. What the Child Should Understand
- **Fair Sharing**: Division models distributing a collection of items fairly so that every container gets the exact same amount.
- **Partitive Structure**: Starting with a known total (e.g. 12 cookies) and a known number of friends (e.g. 3 friends), division finds how many cookies each friend receives.
- **Inverse Relationship**: Division reverses multiplication. If $3 \text{ groups of } 4 = 12$, then sharing $12$ into $3$ groups gives $4$ per group.

---

## 2. What the Child Should Be Able to Do
- Deal items one by one into bowls or baskets until the collection is completely shared.
- Identify the total count, number of groups, and items per group.
- Select or write the matching division sentence ($10 \div 2 = 5$) for a visual sharing set.

---

## 3. Success Criteria & Assessment Rubric
- **Level 1 (Beginning)**: Shares items unequally (e.g., placing 5 on one plate and 3 on another).
- **Level 2 (Developing)**: Shares fairly by dealing items one-by-one, but struggles to state the resulting division equation.
- **Level 3 (Mastered)**: Instantly calculates fair share amounts using inverse multiplication facts, and deals counters evenly.
