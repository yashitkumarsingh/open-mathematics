# Common Misconceptions: Division as Equal Sharing

## 1. Unequal Distribution (Unfair Sharing)
* **The Thinking Trap**: Dumping unequal amounts into containers (e.g. putting 6 on Plate 1 and 2 on Plate 2 when sharing 8 items).
* **Why it Happens**: Focusing only on getting rid of the total pile rather than maintaining equal container amounts.
* **Parent Verbal Correction Prompt**:
  > *"Is that fair sharing? Plate 1 has 6, but Plate 2 only has 2! Take some from Plate 1 and give them to Plate 2 so both have the exact same count."*

---

## 2. Reversing Dividend and Divisor
* **The Thinking Trap**: Writing $3 \div 12 = 4$ instead of $12 \div 3 = 4$.
* **Why it Happens**: Listing the smaller number first out of habit.
* **Parent Verbal Correction Prompt**:
  > *"Division starts with the BIG total pile! We start with 12 total cookies, then divide by 3 friends: $12 \div 3 = 4$."*

---

## 3. Dealing Incomplete Rounds
* **The Thinking Trap**: Stopping the dealing process before all items in the starting pile are distributed.
* **Why it Happens**: Losing track of the original starting pile.
* **Parent Verbal Correction Prompt**:
  > *"Check your starting pile — there are still 3 berries left! Keep dealing one to each plate until your starting pile is empty."*
