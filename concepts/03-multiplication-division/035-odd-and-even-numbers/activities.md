# Hands-on Physical Activities: Odd and Even Numbers

## Activity 1: The Buddy Pair March
* **Target Concept**: Pairing counters to check for leftovers.
* **Materials Needed**: 15 coins or buttons.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Give your child a random number of coins (e.g. 11).
  2. Ask them to line them up in pairs of 2, like marching partners.
  3. Look at the end of the line: *"Is there an odd one out?"* (Yes! 11 has 1 leftover).
  4. Declare: *"11 is ODD!"*

---

## Activity 2: Even Team Equalizer
* **Target Concept**: Connecting even numbers to equal halves.
* **Materials Needed**: 12 small toy cars.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Ask your child to split 12 cars into two equal teams for a race.
  2. Team A gets 6 cars, Team B gets 6 cars. No cars are left out!
  3. Try with 13 cars: Team A gets 6, Team B gets 6, and 1 car is left over.
  4. Reinforce: *"12 can split into equal halves (6 + 6 = EVEN). 13 leaves a leftover (ODD)."*

---

## Activity 3: Flashcard Odd/Even Sorting Race
* **Target Concept**: Fast identification of odd/even by inspecting the ones digit.
* **Materials Needed**: Number cards from 1 to 30.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Create two sorting bins labeled **EVEN (0, 2, 4, 6, 8)** and **ODD (1, 3, 5, 7, 9)**.
  2. Flip cards one by one (e.g. 18, 23, 14, 29).
  3. Have your child shout *"EVEN!"* or *"ODD!"* and drop the card into the correct bin as fast as they can!
