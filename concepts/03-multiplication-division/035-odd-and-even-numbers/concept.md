# Concept Blueprint: Odd and Even Numbers

## 1. What the Child Should Understand
- **Pairing Rule**: Even numbers can be grouped into pairs of 2 with zero leftovers. Odd numbers leave exactly 1 lone item out.
- **Equal Halves**: An even number can be split into two equal whole number groups ($6 = 3 + 3$). An odd number cannot ($7 = 3 + 4$).
- **Pattern Awareness**: Any whole number ending in 0, 2, 4, 6, or 8 is even. Any whole number ending in 1, 3, 5, 7, or 9 is odd.

---

## 2. What the Child Should Be Able to Do
- Pair physical or visual objects side-by-side to check if there is a leftover item.
- Classify a given collection or numeral as Odd or Even.
- Predict whether the sum of two equal groups will be odd or even (it is always even!).

---

## 3. Success Criteria & Assessment Rubric
- **Level 1 (Beginning)**: Guesses odd/even based on memory without checking if items can be paired.
- **Level 2 (Developing)**: Pairs small quantities (up to 10) correctly, but stumbles on two-digit numbers (e.g. 17 or 24).
- **Level 3 (Mastered)**: Instantly identifies odd vs even for numbers up to 30 by inspecting the ones digit, and explains the pairing rule clearly.
