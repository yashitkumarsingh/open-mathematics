# Common Misconceptions: Odd and Even Numbers

## 1. Tens Digit Distraction
* **The Thinking Trap**: Classifying a two-digit number by its tens digit instead of its ones digit (e.g. thinking 27 is even because 2 is even).
* **Why it Happens**: The child reads left-to-right and stops at the first digit they see.
* **Parent Verbal Correction Prompt**:
  > *"Always look at the VERY LAST digit on the right! 27 ends in 7. Is 7 odd or even? 7 has an odd leftover, so 27 is odd!"*

---

## 2. Zero Classification Confusion
* **The Thinking Trap**: Believing zero is neither odd nor even, or assuming 10 is odd because 1 is odd.
* **Why it Happens**: Zero represents nothingness, making it hard to visualize physical pairs.
* **Parent Verbal Correction Prompt**:
  > *"Look at 10 — it ends in 0. Can 10 be split into two equal teams of 5 with no leftovers? Yes! So numbers ending in 0 (like 10 and 20) are EVEN."*

---

## 3. Confusing "Half" with "Pair"
* **The Thinking Trap**: Confusing dividing by 2 with subtracting 2.
* **Why it Happens**: Blending vocabulary words like "two", "second", and "pair".
* **Parent Verbal Correction Prompt**:
  > *"Pairing means finding a buddy for every single item. If every item has a buddy, it's EVEN!"*
