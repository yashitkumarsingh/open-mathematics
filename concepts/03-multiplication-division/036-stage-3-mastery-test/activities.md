# Hands-on Physical Activities: Stage 3 Mixed Mastery Test

## Activity 1: Stage 3 Flashcard Challenge
* **Target Concept**: Mixed recall of Stage 3 skills.
* **Materials Needed**: Flashcards with Stage 3 questions.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Mix cards covering skip counting, equal groups, arrays, division, and odd/even.
  2. Have your child draw a card and answer using physical counters.
  3. Earn a star badge for every 3 cards completed!

---

## Activity 2: The Master Builder Board Game
* **Target Concept**: Applying multiplication, division, and parity rules.
* **Materials Needed**: A homemade paper board game path, 1 die, toy tokens.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Roll the die and move forward.
  2. Land on a space with a math task (e.g., *"Build 2 rows of 4"*, *"Is 15 Odd or Even?"*, *"Share 8 into 2 plates"*).
  3. Reach the Finish space to graduate Stage 3!
