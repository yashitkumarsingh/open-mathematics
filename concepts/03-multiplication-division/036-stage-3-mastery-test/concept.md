# Concept Blueprint: Stage 3 Mixed Mastery Test

## 1. What the Child Should Understand
- **Comprehensive Stage 3 Integration**: Multiplication ($N \times M$), division ($X \div Y$), arrays, skip counting, and odd/even parity are interconnected ways of working with equal groups.
- **Problem Solving Fluency**: Switching flexibly between visual models (number lines, equal group baskets, array grids) based on the problem context.

---

## 2. What the Child Should Be Able to Do
- Complete skip counting jump challenges ($2, 5, 10$).
- Construct equal groups and calculate total items.
- Identify rectangular array dimensions ($\text{rows} \times \text{columns}$).
- Perform fair share division ($A \div B$).
- Classify numbers as odd or even.

---

## 3. Success Criteria & Assessment Rubric
- **Level 1 (Beginning)**: Needs hints and struggles when switching between multiplication and division questions.
- **Level 2 (Developing)**: Solves individual questions accurately but makes minor calculation errors when unassisted.
- **Level 3 (Mastered)**: Scores 5/5 on mixed challenge items fluently, demonstrating full mastery of Stage 3 concepts.
