# Common Misconceptions: Stage 3 Mixed Mastery Test

## 1. Operation Confusion (Adding vs Multiplying)
* **The Thinking Trap**: Adding $3 + 4 = 7$ when shown 3 groups of 4 objects instead of multiplying $3 \times 4 = 12$.
* **Why it Happens**: Defaulting to addition when seeing small numbers.
* **Parent Verbal Correction Prompt**:
  > *"Look at the groups! Are we adding 3 and 4 together, or do we have 3 WHOLE groups of 4 items each?"*

---

## 2. Division & Multiplication Order Confusion
* **The Thinking Trap**: Confusing $15 \div 3 = 5$ with $3 \times 5 = 15$ when reading word prompts.
* **Why it Happens**: Mixed problem sets require reading carefully to identify whether we are combining or sharing.
* **Parent Verbal Correction Prompt**:
  > *"Are we building groups (multiplication) or sharing a total pile into bowls (division)?"*

---

## 3. Parity Misclassification
* **The Thinking Trap**: Forgetting to inspect the ones digit on two-digit mastery items.
* **Why it Happens**: Rushing through the test options.
* **Parent Verbal Correction Prompt**:
  > *"Slow down and check the last digit on the right!"*
