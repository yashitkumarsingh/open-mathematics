# Parent Guide: Stage 3 Mixed Mastery Test

## 2-Minute Concept Overview
This cumulative mastery test evaluates your child's confidence across all of **Stage 3 (Multiplication & Division)**. It mixes skip counting, equal groups, rectangular arrays, fair-share division, and odd/even classification.

---

## 5-Minute Everyday Activity: "Math Stage 3 Challenge Wheel"
- **Materials**: Paper, pencil, dice.
- **How to Play**:
  1. Roll a die to pick a challenge type:
     - **Roll 1**: Skip count by 5s up to 25.
     - **Roll 2**: Build an equal group set (3 plates of 4 crackers).
     - **Roll 3**: Build an array (2 rows of 5).
     - **Roll 4**: Share 10 grapes equally between 2 people.
     - **Roll 5 or 6**: Pick a card and tell if it is ODD or EVEN!
  2. Celebrate every correct response to build confidence!

---

## 10-Minute Extension Challenge: "The Multiplication & Division Story"
- Tell a mini story problem:
  > *"3 squirrels each gathered 4 acorns. How many acorns total?"* ($3 \times 4 = 12$)
- Reverse it:
  > *"They put all 12 acorns in a pile and shared them equally among 3 trees. How many per tree?"* ($12 \div 3 = 4$)
- Ask: *"Is 12 odd or even?"* (**Even**).

---

## Parent Observation Guide
- **Observing test mode**: In Test mode, hints are disabled and progress is logged silently. Keep a calm, encouraging environment!
- **Review mistakes**: Use mistake patterns logged in the parent dashboard to see which specific Stage 3 concept needs quick practice.

---

## What NOT to Do
- ❌ **Don't show anxiety or time pressure.** Let your child take as much time as needed.
- ❌ **Don't criticize wrong answers.** Mark it down silently for review and praise their effort!
