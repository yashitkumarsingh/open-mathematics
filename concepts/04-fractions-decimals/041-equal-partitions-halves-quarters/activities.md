# Physical Hands-On Activities: Equal Partitions & Halves/Quarters

## Activity 1: Playdough Bakery 🥧
* **Materials**: Playdough, plastic knife or ruler.
* **Setup**: Roll out 3 playdough pancakes.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Slice Pancake 1 down the middle into **2 equal halves**.
  2. Slice Pancake 2 into **3 equal thirds**.
  3. Slice Pancake 3 into **4 equal quarters**.
  4. Mix up the slices and have your child reassemble each whole pie!

---

## Activity 2: Is it Fair? Sorting Game ⚖️
* **Materials**: Index cards with drawn shapes (some split equally, some split unequally).
* **Setup**: Place 2 sorting buckets marked **"FAIR (Fractions)"** and **"UNFAIR (Not Fractions)"**.
* **How to Play**:
  1. Have your child inspect each card.
  2. If all parts are identical in size, place it in **FAIR**.
  3. Explain why: *"This square is fair because all 4 quarters match!"*
