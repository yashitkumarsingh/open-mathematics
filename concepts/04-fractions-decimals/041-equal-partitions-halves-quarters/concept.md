# Concept Blueprint: Equal Partitions & Halves/Quarters

## 1. What the Child Should Understand
- **Equal Sharing (Fair Shares)**: A fraction represents dividing a single whole object or set into identical, equal-sized pieces.
- **Vocabulary of Partitions**:
  - **Halves ($1/2$)**: 2 equal parts.
  - **Thirds ($1/3$)**: 3 equal parts.
  - **Quarters / Fourths ($1/4$)**: 4 equal parts.
- **Fairness Principle**: If the parts are unequal in size, they are NOT fractions!

---

## 2. What the Child Should Be Able to Do
- Identify whether a shape is partitioned into equal or unequal parts.
- Match visual shaded fractions (1 out of 2, 1 out of 4) to fraction words ("half", "quarter").
- Partition physical shapes (paper circles, playdough pizzas) into 2 or 4 equal slices.

---

## 3. Success Criteria & Assessment Rubric
- **Level 1 (Beginning)**: Counts the total pieces but ignores whether the pieces are equal in size.
- **Level 2 (Developing)**: Identifies halves and quarters of symmetrical shapes, but hesitates with irregular partitions.
- **Level 3 (Mastered)**: Fluently recognizes equal partitions, identifies halves/thirds/quarters, and connects parts to the whole.
