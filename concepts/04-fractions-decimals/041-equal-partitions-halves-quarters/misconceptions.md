# Common Misconceptions: Equal Partitions & Halves/Quarters

### 1. Ignoring Part Size Equality
* **The Trap**: Calling a shape divided into 4 random unequal parts "fourths" or "quarters".
* **Why it happens**: Focusing solely on counting 4 pieces while ignoring area size.
* **Parent Verbal Prompt**: *"Would it be fair if you got the big slice and I got the tiny slice? Equal parts must be identical in size!"*

---

### 2. More Pieces Means Bigger Fraction Size
* **The Trap**: Thinking a quarter ($1/4$) is bigger than a half ($1/2$) because $4 > 2$.
* **Why it happens**: Whole number bias ($4$ is larger than $2$).
* **Parent Verbal Prompt**: *"When you share a pizza among 4 people, each piece gets smaller! 1 half is bigger than 1 quarter."*

---

### 3. Confusing Cuts and Partitions
* **The Trap**: Thinking 2 line cuts make 2 pieces instead of 3 pieces.
* **Why it happens**: Counting the boundary lines instead of the regions inside.
* **Parent Verbal Prompt**: *"Count the colored rooms inside the house, not the walls!"*
