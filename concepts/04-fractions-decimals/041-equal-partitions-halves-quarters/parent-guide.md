# Parent Guide: Equal Partitions & Halves/Quarters

## 2-Minute Overview
Welcome to **Fractions**! Children start learning fractions by folding paper shapes and slicing food into **equal parts**. The magic word for fractions is **FAIR**: a fraction only exists if every slice is exactly the same size.

---

## 5-Minute Everyday Activity: Pizza Slicing Challenge 🍕
1. Draw a circle on paper or bake a small mini-pizza.
2. Ask your child: *"Cut this pizza so 2 people get a fair share!"* (Halves)
3. Ask: *"Now cut those halves again so 4 people get a fair share!"* (Quarters)
4. Compare a quarter slice to a half slice: *"Which piece is bigger? A half or a quarter?"*

---

## 10-Minute Extension Challenge: Fold-and-Find Paper Fractions 📄
- Take 3 square pieces of paper:
  - Fold Paper 1 in half once $\rightarrow$ **Halves ($1/2$)**
  - Fold Paper 2 in half twice $\rightarrow$ **Quarters ($1/4$)**
  - Fold Paper 3 into 3 equal accordion strips $\rightarrow$ **Thirds ($1/3$)**
- Have your child shade 1 part on each paper and label it!

---

## Parent Observation Guide
- **What to look for**: Does your child think 4 slices means quarters even if one slice is huge and one is tiny? Remind them: *"Fractions must be 100% fair and equal!"*

---

## What NOT to Do
- 🚫 **Do not rush to formal fraction math ($+$, $-$)**: Build strong visual intuition for equal parts first.
- 🚫 **Do not confuse number of cuts with number of pieces**: 1 cut makes 2 pieces!
