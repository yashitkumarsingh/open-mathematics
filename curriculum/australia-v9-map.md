# Australian Curriculum V9 Mapping

This document maps the **Open Mathematics Foundation** (Stage 0, 1, and 2) to the Australian Curriculum Version 9.0 Mathematics outcomes for the **Foundation** and **Year 1** levels.

---

## 🔹 Foundation Year Curriculum Outcomes (Stage 0)

### AC9MFN01 (Establish number names and quantities to 20)
* Connect number names, numerals and quantities, including zero, initially up to 10 and then to 20.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `001-counting-0-to-10`, `002-counting-10-to-20`, `004-match-number-quantity`, `009-zero-as-number`

### AC9MFN02 (Subitise small collections)
* Subitise small collections of objects up to 5.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `005-ten-frame-basics`

### AC9MFN03 (Compare and order collections)
* Compare and order collections, and identify collections with more, fewer (less) or the same number of elements.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `003-compare-more-less-equal`, `006-number-line-0-to-10`

### AC9MFN04 (Represent part-whole relationships up to 10)
* Partition and combine collections, and describe part-whole relationships up to 10.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `007-number-bonds-to-5`, `008-number-bonds-to-10`

---

## 🔹 Year 1 Curriculum Outcomes (Stage 1 & 2)

### AC9M1N01 (Establish place value up to 100)
* Establish understanding of place value of numbers up to 100, including identifying tens and ones.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `021-tens-and-ones`, `023-ten-more-ten-less`

### AC9M1N02 (Represent, order, and compare numbers to 100)
* Recognize, represent and order numbers up to 100, and compare them.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `022-comparing-two-digit-numbers`

### AC9M1N03 (Addition and subtraction within 20)
* Solve problems involving addition and subtraction, and represent them using models.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `011-addition-as-combining`, `012-counting-on`

## 🔹 Year 2 Curriculum Outcomes (Stage 3)

### AC9M2N01 (Skip count by twos, fives, and tens)
* Count by twos, fives and tens from any starting point, forward and backward.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `031-skip-counting-2-5-10`

### AC9M2N04 (Represent multiplication and division as equal groups and arrays)
* Represent multiplication and division problems using equal groups, arrays, and repeated addition.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `032-equal-groups`, `033-arrays-and-multiplication`, `034-division-as-equal-sharing`

## 🔹 Year 3 Curriculum Outcomes (Stage 3 Parity)

### AC9M3N01 (Recognise and describe odd and even numbers)
* Recognise, represent and describe odd and even numbers.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `035-odd-and-even-numbers`
