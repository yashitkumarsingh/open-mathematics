# USA Common Core State Standards (CCSS) Mapping

This document maps the **Open Mathematics Foundation** (Stage 0, 1, and 2) to the USA Common Core State Standards for Mathematics at the **Kindergarten (K)** and **Grade 1 (1st)** levels.

---

## 🔹 Kindergarten Domains & Standards (Stage 0)

### 1. Counting & Cardinality (K.CC)
* **K.CC.A.1, K.CC.A.3**: Count to 100 by ones and tens. Write numbers from 0 to 20. Represent a number of objects with a written numeral.
  - **Concepts**: `001-counting-0-to-10`, `002-counting-10-to-20`, `004-match-number-quantity`, `009-zero-as-number`
* **K.CC.B.4**: Understand the relationship between numbers and quantities; connect counting to cardinality.
  - **Concepts**: `001-counting-0-to-10`
* **K.CC.B.5**: Count to answer "how many?" questions about as many as 20 things.
  - **Concepts**: `001-counting-0-to-10`, `002-counting-10-to-20`
* **K.CC.C.6**: Identify whether the number of objects in one group is greater than, less than, or equal to the number of objects in another group.
  - **Concepts**: `003-compare-more-less-equal`
* **K.CC.C.7**: Compare two numbers between 1 and 10 presented as written numerals.
  - **Concepts**: `003-compare-more-less-equal`, `006-number-line-0-to-10`

### 2. Operations & Algebraic Thinking (K.OA)
* **K.OA.A.1**: Represent addition and subtraction with objects, drawings, expressions, or equations.
  - **Concepts**: `007-number-bonds-to-5`, `008-number-bonds-to-10`
* **K.OA.A.3**: Decompose numbers less than or equal to 10 into pairs in more than one way (e.g. 5 = 2 + 3).
  - **Concepts**: `007-number-bonds-to-5`, `008-number-bonds-to-10`
* **K.OA.A.4**: Find the number that makes 10 when added to any number from 1 to 9.
  - **Concepts**: `008-number-bonds-to-10`

## 🔹 Grade 1 Domains & Standards (Stage 1 & 2)

### 1. Operations & Algebraic Thinking (1.OA)
* **1.OA.A.1**: Use addition and subtraction within 20 to solve word problems involving situations of adding to, taking from, putting together, taking apart, and comparing.
  - **Concepts**: `011-addition-as-combining`
* **1.OA.C.5**: Relate counting to addition and subtraction (e.g., by counting on 2 to add 2).
  - **Concepts**: `012-counting-on`
* **1.OA.C.6**: Add and subtract within 20, demonstrating fluency for addition and subtraction within 10.
  - **Concepts**: `012-counting-on`

### 2. Number & Operations in Base Ten (1.NBT)
* **1.NBT.B.2**: Understand that the two digits of a two-digit number represent amounts of tens and ones.
  - **Concepts**: `021-tens-and-ones`
* **1.NBT.B.3**: Compare two two-digit numbers based on meanings of the tens and ones digits, recording the results of comparisons with the symbols >, =, and <.
  - **Concepts**: `022-comparing-two-digit-numbers`
* **1.NBT.C.5**: Given a two-digit number, mentally find 10 more or 10 less than the number, without having to count; explain the reasoning.
  - **Concepts**: `023-ten-more-ten-less`

## 🔹 Grade 2 & Grade 3 Domains & Standards (Stage 3)

### 1. Number & Operations in Base Ten (2.NBT)
* **2.NBT.A.2**: Count within 1000; skip-count by 5s, 10s, and 100s.
  - **Concepts**: `031-skip-counting-2-5-10`

### 2. Operations & Algebraic Thinking (2.OA / 3.OA)
* **2.OA.C.3**: Determine whether a group of objects (up to 20) has an odd or even number of members, e.g., by pairing objects or counting them by 2s.
  - **Concepts**: `035-odd-and-even-numbers`
* **2.OA.C.4**: Use addition to find the total number of objects arranged in rectangular arrays with up to 5 rows and up to 5 columns.
  - **Concepts**: `033-arrays-and-multiplication`
* **3.OA.A.1**: Interpret products of whole numbers, e.g., interpret 5 × 7 as the total number of objects in 5 groups of 7 objects each.
  - **Concepts**: `032-equal-groups`, `033-arrays-and-multiplication`
* **3.OA.A.2**: Interpret whole-number quotients of whole numbers, e.g., interpret 56 ÷ 8 as the number of objects in each share when 56 objects are partitioned equally into 8 shares.
  - **Concepts**: `034-division-as-equal-sharing`
