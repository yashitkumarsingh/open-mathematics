# Indian NCERT Primary Mathematics (Ganita Prakash) Mapping

This document maps the **Open Mathematics Foundation** core concepts (Stage 0 to Stage 3) to the National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT) Mathematics curriculum standards (*Ganita Prakash* & *Math-Magic*) for **Class 1**, **Class 2**, **Class 3**, and **Class 4**.

---

## 🔹 Class 1 NCERT Outcomes (Stage 0 & Stage 1)

### Chapter 1: Finding the Position & Shapes
* Recognize spatial relationships (Inside-Outside, Bigger-Smaller, Top-Bottom).
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `003-compare-more-less-equal`

### Chapter 2: Numbers 1 to 9 & Zero (Sankhyaon Ki Pehchan)
* Count, match, and write digits from 1 to 9. Understand zero (Shoonya) as an empty set.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `001-counting-0-to-10`, `004-match-number-quantity`, `005-ten-frame-basics`, `009-zero-as-number`

### Chapter 3: Addition up to 9 (Jodh)
* Combine two collections of concrete objects to find the total count.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `007-number-bonds-to-5`, `011-addition-as-combining`

### Chapter 4: Subtraction up to 9 (Ghataav)
* Take away items from a set and count the remaining items.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `013-subtraction-as-taking-away`

### Chapter 5: Numbers 10 to 20
* Group 10 items into a bundle (Das ki Gaddi) and count beyond 10.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `002-counting-10-to-20`, `006-number-line-0-to-10`, `008-number-bonds-to-10`

---

## 🔹 Class 2 NCERT Outcomes (Stage 1 & Stage 2)

### Chapter 1: Counting in Groups (Samooh Mein Ginti)
* Grouping items into pairs, tens, and patterns. Counting on from a known number.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `012-counting-on`, `031-skip-counting-2-5-10`

### Chapter 2: Tens and Ones (Dahai Aur Ikai)
* Decompose two-digit numbers into Bundles of Ten (Dahai) and Loose Sticks (Ikai).
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `021-tens-and-ones`, `023-ten-more-ten-less`

### Chapter 3: Comparing Numbers & Subtraction on Scale
* Compare two-digit quantities and model subtraction on number line scales.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `014-subtraction-on-number-line`, `022-comparing-two-digit-numbers`

---

## 🔹 Class 3 NCERT Outcomes (Stage 3)

### Chapter 1: Give and Take (Lene Den Ka Khel)
* Mental addition and subtraction using 10-more/10-less hops on number grids.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `015-mixed-addition-subtraction`, `023-ten-more-ten-less`

### Chapter 2: How Many Times? (Kitni Baar?)
* Repeated addition, equal groups, and rectangular array grids for multiplication.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `032-equal-groups`, `033-arrays-and-multiplication`

### Chapter 3: Can We Share? (Bantwara)
* Fair sharing, equal distribution, and introductory division concepts.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `034-division-as-equal-sharing`

### Chapter 4: Pairs and Odd/Even Numbers
* Identifying pairing patterns and digit parity (Sam aur Visham Sankhyaen).
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `035-odd-and-even-numbers`

---

## 🔹 Class 4 NCERT Outcomes (Stage 3 Parity & Mastery)

### Chapter 1: Building with Bricks & Place Value Expansion
* Expanding numbers into Hundreds, Tens, and Ones ($100s, 10s, 1s$).
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `024-expanded-form-tens`, `025-hundreds-tens-ones`

### Chapter 2: Multi-Step Mastery & Problem Solving
* Synthesized mastery assessments across counting, grouping, operations, and sharing.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `016-stage-1-mastery-test`, `026-stage-2-mastery-test`, `036-stage-3-mastery-test`
