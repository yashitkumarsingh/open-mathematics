# Singapore Primary Mathematics (CPA) Mapping Framework

This document maps the **Open Mathematics Foundation** (OMF) core concepts (Stage 0 to Stage 3) to the Singapore Primary Mathematics **Concrete-Pictorial-Abstract (CPA)** pedagogical framework established by Jerome Bruner and adopted by Singapore's Ministry of Education (MOE).

---

## 📐 1. Overview of the CPA Progression

The Singapore Math CPA approach introduces mathematical concepts across three progressive stages:

1. **Concrete (Hands-on)**: Manipulating physical objects or interactive visual representations (e.g. counters, ten-frames, number rods, place-value bundles).
2. **Pictorial (Visual)**: Representing numbers and relationships visually (e.g. number bonds, bar models, part-whole diagrams, number line scales).
3. **Abstract (Symbolic)**: Using standard numerals and equations ($+$, $-$, $\times$, $\div$, $=$, $>$, $<$).

## 🔹 Primary 1 CPA Standards & OMF Alignment

### Whole Numbers to 20 & Part-Whole Bonds
* **Concrete**: Counting physical blocks; filling `<ten-frame>` counters with dual-color chips.
* **Pictorial**: Number bond diagrams (Part-Part-Whole circles), scale notches on `<number-line>`.
* **Abstract**: Numeral recognition ($0$–$20$), equation sentences ($4 + 3 = 7$, $9 - 4 = 5$).
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `001-counting-0-to-10`, `002-counting-10-to-20`, `004-match-number-quantity`, `005-ten-frame-basics`, `006-number-line-0-to-10`, `007-number-bonds-to-5`, `008-number-bonds-to-10`, `009-zero-as-number`

### Addition & Subtraction Concepts
* **Concrete**: Aggregating sets of counters; clearing/removing counters from ten-frames; dragging items in `<drag-counter>`.
* **Pictorial**: Part-Whole & Comparison Bar Models; backward hops on `<number-line>`.
* **Abstract**: Addition ($a + b = c$), Subtraction ($c - b = a$), Fact Family equivalence.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `011-addition-as-combining`, `012-counting-on`, `013-subtraction-as-taking-away`, `014-subtraction-on-number-line`, `015-mixed-addition-subtraction`, `016-stage-1-mastery-test`

## 🔹 Primary 2 CPA Standards & OMF Alignment

### Place Value & Number Comparisons to 100
* **Concrete**: Bundling 10 straws into Tens rods using `<base-ten-blocks>`; place value mat.
* **Pictorial**: Place value charts; 100-grid highlighting on `<hundred-chart>`.
* **Abstract**: Two-digit expansion ($25 = 20 + 5$), comparison symbols ($>$, $<$, $=$).
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `021-tens-and-ones`, `022-comparing-two-digit-numbers`, `023-ten-more-ten-less`, `024-expanded-form-tens`, `025-hundreds-tens-ones`, `026-stage-2-mastery-test`

### Skip Counting & Equal Groups
* **Concrete**: Arranging counters into equal-sized stacks/baskets.
* **Pictorial**: Highlighting multiples of 2, 5, and 10 on `<hundred-chart>`.
* **Abstract**: Skip-counting sequences ($2, 4, 6, 8...$), equal group notation ($3 \text{ groups of } 4$).
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `031-skip-counting-2-5-10`, `032-equal-groups`

---

## 🔹 Primary 3 CPA Standards & OMF Alignment

### Multiplication, Division & Rectangular Arrays
* **Concrete**: Arranging counters into row $\times$ column rectangular array grids.
* **Pictorial**: Equal-part Bar Models; array grid diagrams.
* **Abstract**: Multiplication sentences ($a \times b = c$), Division as equal sharing ($c \div a = b$).
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `033-arrays-and-multiplication`, `034-division-as-equal-sharing`

### Parity & Stage Mastery
* **Concrete**: Pairing counters to check for leftovers.
* **Pictorial**: Pair-grid visual checks on `<hundred-chart>`.
* **Abstract**: Even vs. Odd digit classification, cumulative stage assessments.
  - **Mapped Concepts**: `035-odd-and-even-numbers`, `036-stage-3-mastery-test`
