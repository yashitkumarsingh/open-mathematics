# Singapore Math (CPA) Curriculum Mapping Framework

This document maps Open Mathematics Framework (OMF) core concepts to the Singapore Primary Mathematics Concrete-Pictorial-Abstract (CPA) pedagogical framework developed by Jerome Bruner and adopted by Singapore's Ministry of Education (MOE).

---

## 📐 1. Overview of the CPA Framework

The **Concrete-Pictorial-Abstract (CPA)** approach is an essential learning model that builds deep conceptual understanding by introducing mathematical concepts in three progressive stages:

1. **Concrete (Hands-on):** Manipulating physical objects (e.g., counters, base-10 blocks, fraction bars).
2. **Pictorial (Visual):** Representing mathematical concepts visually (e.g., bar models, number bonds, diagrams).
3. **Abstract (Symbolic):** Using mathematical symbols, numbers, and equations ($+, -, \times, \div, x, y$).

---

## 📊 2. Concept Mapping Table

| OMF Topic | Concrete Stage | Pictorial Stage (Singapore Bar Model) | Abstract Stage (Symbolic) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Number Sense & Place Value** | Base-10 blocks, place value chips | Number bonds, place value charts | Expanded form, numeral representation |
| **Addition & Subtraction** | Physical counting blocks, interlocking cubes | Part-Whole & Comparison Bar Models | $a + b = c$, $c - a = b$ |
| **Multiplication & Division** | Equal groups of counters, array tiles | Equal-part Bar Models | $n \times m = p$, $p \div n = m$ |
| **Fractions & Ratio** | Fraction strips, pie cutouts | Fractional Bar Models, ratio comparison blocks | $\frac{a}{b}$, $a:b$ ratio expressions |
| **Algebraic Thinking** | Balance scales with weighted blocks | Bar models representing unknown quantities | $x + 5 = 12 \Rightarrow x = 7$ |

---

## 🎯 3. Integration Guidelines for OMF Curriculum

- **Primary 1 – Primary 3:** Prioritize Concrete and Pictorial representations before introducing formal symbolic equations.
- **Primary 4 – Primary 6:** Utilize the Singapore Bar Model method to solve multi-step word problems involving fractions, ratios, and percentages.
- **Secondary Transition:** Transition bar model visualization directly into algebraic variable equations.
