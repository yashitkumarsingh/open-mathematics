🔌 100% Offline-First

Runs locally in your browser. Double-click to play from folders, USB sticks, or low-connectivity classrooms without web servers.

🧠 Neurodiverse Design

Calm colors, zero flashing timers, integrated Text-to-Speech (TTS), dyslexia spacing, and dyspraxia-friendly click fallbacks.

🏡 Parent-Educator Guided

Each lesson incorporates specific physical activities and misconception taxonomy diagnostics to help parents tutor kids effectively.

🛡️ Privacy Respecting

Zero ad networks, no data tracking, no cookies. Your progress is stored locally in your browser with JSON backups.
